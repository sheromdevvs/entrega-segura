# Original visual assets and prompts

All four raster images were created with the built-in image-generation tool for this project, then encoded as WebP for web delivery. No paid image API call or image API key was used. They depict fictional situations, not Pascal customers or an actual courier. DM Sans is locally hosted under the included SIL OFL. The voice sample was locally synthesized with macOS Paulina and is labelled synthetic.

## Delivery editorial

Saved asset: `public/images/delivery-editorial.webp`.

Prompt: Create a premium editorial black-and-white documentary photograph for the hero of Entrega Segura, a delivery payment product for small Ecuadorian personalized gift shops. Landscape 3:2 composition. A close, candid, tactile view at a sunlit apartment doorway in Quito: two people's hands sharing a small unbranded white cardboard parcel, partly open with a carefully folded off-white cotton sweatshirt inside bearing a small black embroidered outline of a dog. Receiver's hand in a dark sleeve on the right, delivery person's hands and plain black overshirt on the left. Only torsos and hands, no faces. Background is a real textured pale plaster wall and a softly blurred metal doorframe. Beautiful hard afternoon window light creates deep black shadows and luminous whites; rich authentic textile and cardboard grain, high-end medium format film photography, restrained photographic grain, quiet human emotion, asymmetrical but balanced crop, lived-in rather than stock-photo posing. Entirely monochrome black/white/neutral gray; no colors, no green, no purple, no UI, no cards, no text, no logos, no watermark. The parcel is the focal point in the central lower half and hands feel natural and anatomically correct.

## Made with care

Saved asset: `public/images/made-with-care.webp`.

Prompt: A premium editorial black-and-white photograph, vertical portrait 4:5, for a small personalized-gift shop payment product. Top-down close crop of a small working table: the hands of a craftsperson in a plain black cotton shirt carefully wrapping a white ceramic mug in translucent tissue paper, next to an open matte-white gift carton, short pieces of black ribbon and fabric sample with a tiny line drawing of a dog. Strong diagonal natural sunlight across an off-white textured table; a quiet real small-business workshop rather than a polished generic stock-photo studio. Impeccable contrast, soft film grain, tactile paper, ceramics and cotton; honest human craft, real hands, nuanced silver grays, no color whatsoever. No faces, no readable text, no typography, no logos, no watermarks, no screens, no credit cards. Compose hands and mug centrally with cropped table edges for a close intimate photograph, timeless and confident.

## Pet frame

Saved asset: `public/images/pet-frame.webp`.

Prompt: Black-and-white editorial product photograph, square composition, for a small Ecuadorian personalized gift shop. A simple matte black 8x10 inch picture frame standing on a pale plaster shelf, holding a charming detailed monochrome line portrait of a small scruffy dog on white archival paper. No text or names. Beside the frame lies a neatly folded white tissue wrapper and a small unbranded white carton corner. Natural side light, deep black shadows, architectural real texture, beautiful realistic paper and matte frame, subtly grainy medium-format film character. Quiet, warm in feeling despite monochrome, intentional human craft rather than generic ecommerce render. Tight crop, frame occupies about two thirds of the image, no flowers, no decorative foliage, no green, no colors, no branding, no price, no lettering, no watermark. Photorealistic, gallery-quality still life.

## Fictional defect evidence

Saved asset: `public/images/defect-evidence.webp`.

Prompt: A realistic black-and-white documentary evidence photo for a FICTIONAL demo of a damaged personalized gift. Square crop. A simple white ceramic mug on a white tabletop beside torn white tissue wrapping and a plain dark cardboard carton, with one clearly visible thin hairline crack running down from the rim and a small chipped rim fragment beside it. Mug is empty, no liquid, no hand, no brand, no lettering. Natural window light, honest smartphone-style overhead perspective rather than a pristine product render, detailed ceramic and cardboard texture, clear focus on the crack, restrained contrast. Entirely monochrome, no green or any color, no watermark, no UI. This synthetic image is to demonstrate a defect report only.
